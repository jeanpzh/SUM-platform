## Verified transition and equivalency evidence

### Plan 2009 transition

- **DOC-130, Acta Consejo de Facultad Virtual No. 17, Sesión Ordinaria 12, 22 Sep 2020, pp.2–5:** The immediate proposal to leave Plan 2009 without effect in 2020 was rejected (p.3); a later motion applying closure from 2022-I was accepted with 8 votes and 1 abstention (p.5). This supports the plan end after 2021-II. The acta says RR 00698-R-20 already approved a Plan 2009→2014 equivalency table (p.2), but that RR/table is not acquired.
- **DOC-118, official FISI notice:** reports the cycle groups I–VI→2018, VII–VIII→2014, IX–X finish under 2009. Those detailed routes do not appear in the acquired Acta 17 text.
- **DOC-128, Acta 23 (20 Jul 2021), p.4:** the EPIS director asks that the 2009→2018 equivalency table be considered at an extraordinary Council meeting.
- **DOC-129, Acta 24 (27 Jul 2021), pp.2–3:** describes proposed migration (cycles I–VI→2018; VII–VIII→2014; IX–X remain 2009) and proposed 2009→2018 table, then states both items remained pending approval. It cannot be cited as the operative approval of those later proposals.
- **Unresolved:** how the FISI notice's cycle routes were authorized and whether a subsequent Council/Decanal/Rectoral action adopted the 2021 proposal/table. The exact RD 000510-D-FISI-19 and RR 00721-R-20 remain missing.

### Plan 2014→2018

- **DOC-122 RD 000609-2022-D-FISI, p.1:** operative clause 1 approves an equivalency table from the Systems Engineering 2014 plan to the 2018 plan. Its considerations cite RR 02137-R-15 for Plan 2014 and RR 07026-R-17 / RR 06615-R-18 / RR 01529-R-19 for Plan 2018 and its amendments.

## University-wide interpretive rules acquired

- **Current matrícula:** DOC-005 + DOC-002 (RR 002642-2026-R and 18-page regulation) apply to pregrado. Regulation Article 31 places reactualized students on the current study plan. Definitions distinguish current plan from a plan in extinction; the latter is restricted to students admitted during its validity who continue without interruption. Article 30 bars reactualization in an established abandonment case. An older plan alone does not determine eligibility; school, assigned plan, interruption/abandonment status and semester matter.
- **Convalidation vs plan equivalency:** DOC-086 RR 005516-2021-R defines pregrado convalidation, including 80% syllabus overlap and credits equal to or greater than destination course; its reingreso definition is a student who lost status and re-enters by admission competition, and is distinct from reactualization. DOC-033 Directiva SUM 008-2012 requests a Rectoral-approved plan-equivalency table on plan changes, but its 2012 base references an older matrícula regulation; current applicability requires comparison.
- **Plan registration:** DOC-022 Directiva SUM 001-2021 says plans must be ratified by Rectoral Resolution and include prerequisites, equivalencies where needed, and total credits (clauses 1–2). This explains what approval records to seek; it does not supply the missing FISI plans.
- **Language:** DOC-126 RR 014041-2024-R and its directive (Art. 3, Art. 4(a), Art. 4(g), Art. 5.4) cover students who started from the effective Ley 30220 period; basic-level language evidence is required for degree/title, while students passing foreign-language courses within their plans are deemed to have accredited it. FISI's undated DOC-103 checklist calls the certificate optional; it is not a safe replacement for the university directive.
- **Degree research work:** DOC-127 RR 00744-R-20 approves a general research/thesis/professional-sufficiency directive (scope described p.1 as students who entered from 2016 or were incorporated into plans for that reference year). The annex pages 3–18 are image-only and require OCR; detailed requirements remain unverified.
- **Automatic bachelor transition:** DOC-083/RR 003078-2022 modifies the second transitional provision to cover study completion years 2020–2023, through 2023-II. It is historical and not a general current cohort exemption.
- **Evaluation:** DOC-117 RR 007510-2021 states scope as plans 2019 and earlier. Application to Systems 2023, Software 2018/2023, Computing 2023 and AI is not established by this rule alone.
