# FISI study plans and transitions — evidence review

**Research date:** 2026-09-29 (the browser capture timestamps are 2026-09-30 UTC).  
**Method:** Primary FISI/UNMSM and gob.pe pages opened in Playwright and inspected with accessibility snapshots. Search-engine results were used only to discover primary pages and are not treated as validity evidence. The original inventory and dictionary were read as evidence to review, not as instructions.

## Executive findings

1. The current official FISI undergraduate navigation lists four EP labels: Inteligencia Artificial (“new”), Ingeniería de Sistemas, Ingeniería de Software and Ciencia de la Computación. The current Systems page profile confirms Systems Engineering as a 5-year/10-semester program. This does not establish which approved plans or cohorts apply.
2. FISI’s official Systems Engineering notice describes the 2009 plan as in force only through 2021-II and provides cycle-based routes: cycles 1–6 to 2018, cycles 7–8 to 2014, cycles 9–10 may finish under 2009. It does not give admission years or an explicit compulsory/optional migration rule.
3. A FISI official resolution-category search result independently surfaced a clause reference that RR 00721-R-20 ratified RD 000510-D-FISI-19, which had approved leaving the 2009 plan without validity. The notice and the resolution reference require reconciliation against the underlying instruments and exact operative clauses. The 2020 notice’s 2021-II completion arrangement should not be erased by a blanket “superseded” flag.
4. The official gob.pe RR 000046-2026-R record and signed attachment establish that the “Tabla de Equivalencia Plan 2018-Plan 2023” covers EP Ingeniería de Sistemas: the attachment page 1 *Visto* names the school, and page 2 contains paired Plan 2018/Plan 2023 DICE/DEBE DECIR tables. The record names RDs 000777-2024-D-FISI (27 Jun 2024), expanded by RD 001030-2024-D-FISI (6 Sep 2024), with modifications ratified through RDs 000738-2025-D-FISI (19 Jun 2025) and 001281-2025-D-FISI (21 Nov 2025). The page-2 table is visually inspected but still requires manual row/cell verification before ingestion; it corrects specific rows, not every course in the plans.
5. FISI’s postgraduate catalog lists 2 doctorates and 5 master’s programs at capture. Its “Planes de Estudio” page is marketing/discovery content, not an approved curriculum set: it does not expose resolution IDs, cohorts, course codes, credits, prerequisites or transition rules. All those fields remain **UNKNOWN**.
6. In the supplied inventory, the SUM study-plan discovery endpoints are already recorded as source gaps, but FISI and official Gob.pe URLs above are new source records. No plan can be declared superseded solely because a newer plan exists.

## Systems Engineering 2009-plan notice and transition evidence

**Official notice:** [Comunicado sobre la vigencia del Plan de Estudios 2009 de la Escuela Profesional de Ingeniería de Sistemas](https://sistemas.unmsm.edu.pe/site/noticias/35-importante/188-comunicado-sobre-la-vigencia-del-plan-de-estudios-2009-de-la-escuela-profesional-de-ingenieria-de-sistemas). It reports that the Faculty Council Virtual No. 17, Ordinary Session No. 12, on 22 Sep 2020 approved that the 2009 plan would be in force only until academic semester 2021-II. The notice states:

| Student progress in the 2009 plan | Notice’s stated route |
|---|---|
| 1st–6th cycle | Could (“podrán”) convalidate courses to Plan 2018 |
| 7th–8th cycle | Could convalidate courses to Plan 2014 |
| 9th–10th cycle | Could finish studies under Plan 2009 |

**Not established by the notice:** admission year of any group; compulsory versus optional migration; a course-by-course equivalency table; course discontinuation / offerings after 2021-II; return-after-interruption rule; whether students outside those stated cycle groups retain rights; which specific cohorts were individually assigned plans. All are **UNKNOWN**.

**Council minutes acquired:** [Acta Consejo de Facultad Virtual No. 17, Sesión Ordinaria 12](https://sistemas.unmsm.edu.pe/site/rd?task=download.send&id=16&catid=5&m=0), dated 22 Sep 2020, is stored as `sources/originals/FISI-Acta-Consejo-Facultad-2020-09-22-virtual17-ordinaria12.pdf` with page-labelled extraction. Pages 2–5 show the Council rejected immediate closure of Plan 2009 (8 no, 1 abstention) and accepted 8–1 a motion to leave it without effect from 2022-I; this supports the notice’s 2021-II end date. Page 2 says RR 00698-R-20 had approved a Plan 2009–2014 equivalency table. The exact RR 00698 PDF/table, RD 000510-D-FISI-19 and RR 00721-R-20 remain unacquired.

**Cycle-route limitation:** the 2020 acta does not establish the FISI notice’s cycle-group destination routes. The later [Acta CF Virtual No. 24, Session 14 (27 Jul 2021)](https://sistemas.unmsm.edu.pe/site/rd?task=download.send&id=146&catid=6&m=0), stored as DOC-129, records a proposal using those cycle groups and a 2009→2018 equivalency table, but pp.2–3 say both items remained pending approval at that meeting. A later adoption instrument/table was not located. Do not present the 2021 proposal as operative or infer the individual student’s plan from admission year.

The notice’s destination plans (2014 and 2018) are not accompanied by the approval resolutions, full curricular texts, or equivalency annexes on the notice page. Those sources remain **UNKNOWN**.

## Review guardrails

- Keep separate rows for resolution, annex, notice, plan text and equivalency attachment.
- Preserve source/discovery URL for every row; never fill an unavailable direct resolution URL from a guessed filename.
- Keep adoption/approval date, publication/upload date, academic coverage period, admission year, plan assignment and transition dates in separate fields.
- Do not label a base plan “superseded” based only on a newer plan. Require an explicit operative clause and account for completion/returning-student provisions.
- Keep undergraduate and postgraduate materials in different collections and matrix sections.
