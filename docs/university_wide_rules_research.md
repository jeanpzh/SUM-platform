# University-wide rules relevant to FISI students

**Evidence checked:** 2026-09-29 (Playwright captures mostly timestamped 2026-09-30 UTC).  
**Starting point:** `data/document_inventory.csv`, `docs/document_dictionary.md`, and the prior FISI plan review. Existing records were treated as leads, not authority.  
**Method:** Official SUM, UNMSM/FISI, UNMSM Gob.pe, VRAP and faculty official pages opened in Playwright and inspected with accessibility snapshots. PDF attachments were opened in Chrome’s PDF viewer and page screenshots reviewed for RR 002642-2026-R, its 18-page enrollment annex, and RR 003078-2022-R. Search-result snippets only discovered official links; they are not used as final legal authority.  
**Scope limitation:** This is an evidence coverage review, not a complete legal consolidation of every cited instrument. Unsupported clause details, publication/effective dates, cohort scope or conflict outcomes are marked **UNKNOWN** or “not verified”; the original inventory should retain historical records.

## Executive summary

- **Enrollment:** RR 002642-2026-R approves the 18-folio *Reglamento General de Matrícula para Pregrado*. Its clause 1 makes it effective the day after issuance (issued 25 Feb 2026; effective 26 Feb); clause 2 disapplies provisions contrary to that RR, rather than expressly naming every predecessor as wholly repealed. The regulation’s Art. 3 applies to all undergraduate students.
- **Procedures and authority:** The 2026 annex has chapters for enrollment, rectification and reactivation. Arts. 26–29 establish rectification request/report, faculty Vicedecano authorization, SUM execution, and justified ex officio rectification within the calendar window. Arts. 30–33 require advance reactualization, place a returning student on the school’s current study plan (Art.31), specify supported reactivation grounds (Art.32), and require the Faculty Academic VP to send approved resolutions to SUM within the official calendar window (Art.33).
- **Deadline separation:** SUM’s 2026-II schedule states that the 3 Aug date for reactualization/reservation is the **deadline for the faculty dean’s resolution to reach SUM**. It explicitly leaves each faculty to establish an earlier student filing deadline. The master calendar shows regular student-facing rectification/late-registration dates as 7–11 Sep 2026 and special rectification (Faculty Council agreement) as 19–23 Oct. The FISI notice and its linked forms announce the process but display no application dates in the page text reviewed. Do not use 3 Aug as a student deadline.
- **Evaluation and plan scope:** RR 007510-2021 is described by its official annex as applying to undergraduate students in study plans **2019 and earlier**. The enrollment 2026 regulation cites it in its legal basis, but does not thereby expand RR 007510’s stated plan scope. Coverage of students on newer plans is unresolved.
- **Academic standing:** The official consolidated Law 30220 text (updated collection 13 Aug 2025), Arts. 99.8 and 100.11, sets a 12-credit regular-status minimum with a completion exception and caps reservation at three consecutive or alternate years. Art. 102 states temporary separation for one year after the third failure of a course, return limited to that course, and definitive withdrawal after the fourth; its following paragraph expressly permits a university Statute to impose automatic definitive separation on the third failure. This makes Estatuto Art.189 and RR 04005-R-16 essential to resolve, not optional historical context.
- **Convalidation/equivalency:** University-level RR 005516-2021 is the base regulation cited in the 2026 enrollment annex. Foreign-university recognition RR 004915-2025, SUM Directiva 007 (convalidation) and Directiva 008 (equivalency) cover potentially different procedures. Their scopes and any conflict/repeal relationships are not yet reconciled.
- **Degrees/titles/language:** Current consolidated Law 30220 Art.45.1 requires approved undergraduate study and foreign-language knowledge (preferably English) or a native language; as amended by Law 31803 it includes a research-course in the final semester. Art.45.2 requires bachelor plus thesis or professional-sufficiency work for professional title. Master/doctorate credit, duration, thesis and language requirements are in Arts.45.4–45.5. The same current law presents transitional completion rules through 31 Mar 2024 (research-work exception) and an implementation window to 31 Mar 2025 for a described graduate group. Separately, the 2022 RR extension covers 2020–2023 including 2023-II. These are completion-period rules, not admission-year mappings; preserve both and verify a person’s eligibility rather than generalizing a blanket language waiver.
- **Internships/services:** The inventory has university-wide tutoring and mental-health leads but no verified UNMSM-wide internship regulation. Search results for practice regulations led to other faculties’ rules; those must not be applied to FISI. No private student account was accessed, no form was submitted, and no staff were contacted.

## Unresolved conflicts and source gaps

1. **Repeal/effectivity:** compare full RR 002642-2026 and 18-folio annex with RR006376-2023 / RR002891-2025. Clause 2 addresses contradictory provisions; no full-instrument repeal is asserted absent explicit operative language.
2. **Student vs faculty deadline:** FISI needs to publish its actual student filing period for reactivation/reservation and any 2026-II form deadline. SUM’s 3 Aug is expressly dean’s resolution delivery to SUM.
3. **Rectification and reservation:** compare annex Arts.13(d–k), 26–33 with SUM Directivas 005, 006, 009, 010. Resolve reservation maximum wording (three consecutive years vs statutory consecutive-or-alternate years), statutory grounds vs faculty approval, and directive approval mapping. Record Art.31 current-plan placement as a possible transition issue for legacy-plan returning students.
4. **Plan-restricted evaluation:** RR007510-2021 applies to plans 2019 and earlier; locate governing assessment instrument for plans 2020/2023/newer FISI cohorts and map FISI syllabi/assessment notices separately.
5. **Repeated failures and academic standing:** current law PDF p.53 Art.102 and enrollment annex Arts.13(j–k) are reviewed; retrieve full Statute RR03013-R-16 Art.189 and RR04005-R-16. Art.102 itself reserves room for Statute-level automatic separation on failure three; RGM Art.13(k) states low-performance separation under Art.102 prohibits readmission. Separate this route from non-enrollment abandonment (Art.13(j)) and establish each temporal/cohort operation.
6. **Convalidation/equivalence:** read RR005516-2021, RR004915-2025, Directiva 007/D7 and Directiva 008/D8. Determine overlap, applicable level/plan, credit threshold, deadlines, authority and appeal/review rules; do not conflate foreign recognition with internal plan equivalency.
7. **Degrees/language:** current law PDF pp.26–28 and Art.45 requirements are reviewed; still inspect complete base RGT + RR004503-2021/RR003078-2022 transition clauses and official language-certification procedures. Confirm accepted proof/level and exact eligible graduate population/date cutoffs. Treat RR001273-2023 revalidation separately.
8. **Internships:** find the applicable national practice law and any faculty-wide FISI Council/Decanal/Rectoral rule plus the specific school-plan clause; do not use another faculty’s regulations as evidence.
9. **Student services:** inspect RR014348-2024 annex and RR for mental-health regulation; map FISI UNAYOE services and any student-risk/retention programs with their own temporal limits.
10. **Access:** older SUM directivas and historic RR files remain relevant for past records and should remain in archive/search. Mark validity and applicability UNKNOWN until text comparison; index presence alone does not establish current status.
