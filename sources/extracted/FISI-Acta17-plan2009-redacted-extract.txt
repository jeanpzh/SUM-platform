## p.2 — request to end the 2009 plan

The agenda item is “Solicita dejar sin efecto el Plan de Estudios 2009 de la EPIS.” It reports the Systems EP request to end Plan 2009 and cites RR 00698-R-20 (18 February 2020) as approving an equivalency table for Plans 2009–2014. A council member requests extending Plan 2009 through 2021-II because the proposed equivalence reportedly omitted courses and could cost students credits.

## p.3 — first vote

The Council votes on the proposal to leave Plan 2009 without effect immediately. The proposal is **rejected: 8 votes against, 1 abstention**.

## pp.4–5 — delayed end date

The Council discusses ending Plan 2009 after semester 2021-II. On p.5 the motion is worded as leaving Plan 2009 without effect and applying that decision in semester 2022-I. The motion is **accepted: 8 votes in favor, 1 abstention**.

## Evidentiary limit

This Acta supports the end-date vote and that RR 00698-R-20's Plan 2009→2014 equivalency table was cited in the meeting. It does not supply RR 00698 or that table, does not establish the cycle-group routes shown in the separate FISI notice, and does not establish student-specific assigned plans.
